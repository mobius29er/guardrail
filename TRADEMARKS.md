# Halligan Trademark Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

## Purpose

Halligan is open-source software licensed under the Apache License, Version
2.0. Foxxception LLC wants Halligan to be widely used, integrated, discussed,
extended, and implemented.

This policy protects the Halligan name and logo as reliable indicators of the
official project while permitting broad, truthful references to Halligan and
compatibility with Halligan.

The Apache License, Version 2.0 governs use, modification, and distribution of
the Halligan software. This policy governs use of the Halligan Marks. It does
not limit any rights granted by the Apache License or applicable law.

## The Halligan Marks

The “Halligan Marks” are the Halligan™ word mark and the official Halligan
project logo, as used by Foxxception LLC to identify the Halligan AI guardrail
durability testing project and its software.

## Uses allowed without permission

You may use the Halligan name without requesting permission to:

- Truthfully refer to the Halligan open-source project
- Link to [halligan.dev](https://halligan.dev/) or the official source
  repository
- State that your software uses, integrates with, supports, or is compatible
  with Halligan
- State that your software is based on or derived from Halligan
- Describe your implementation as “Halligan-compatible” when that statement is
  accurate
- Publish tutorials, reviews, research, comparisons, benchmarks,
  documentation, books, articles, presentations, or training about Halligan
- Distribute unmodified copies of official Halligan releases while identifying
  the original version and preserving the required license and attribution
- Identify yourself as a Halligan contributor or community member when that
  statement is accurate

Acceptable examples include:

- “Compatible with Halligan”
- “Built with Halligan”
- “Uses the Halligan guardrail testing framework”
- “Based on the Halligan open-source project”
- “Produces reports compatible with the identified Halligan version”
- “Independent training for teams using Halligan”

These permissions are subject to truthful use, compliance with the conditions
below, and presentation that does not create a likelihood of confusion about
source, sponsorship, affiliation, certification, or endorsement.

## Integrations and extensions

You may use Halligan as a secondary, descriptive component of an integration
or extension name, such as “Acme Halligan Adapter,” provided your own name is
dominant and the project clearly states that it is independently maintained.

Written permission is required when Halligan is used as the primary
source-identifying name of a company, product, hosted service, package, domain,
event, or commercial offering, or when the name otherwise suggests official
status.

## Modified versions and forks

The Apache License permits modified distributions and forks. This policy does
not restrict those software-license rights.

If a modified distribution uses the Halligan name, it must clearly identify
itself as modified and independently maintained. A modified distribution
should use its own primary product name and visual identity and may truthfully
state that it is “based on Halligan,” “derived from Halligan,” or “compatible
with Halligan.”

It must not present itself as an official Halligan release or use the official
Halligan logo as its primary branding without written permission.

A modified distribution should include a clear statement such as:

> This product is based on the Halligan open-source project and is not
> produced, endorsed, or supported by Foxxception LLC.

## “Tested with Halligan”

You may use the phrase “Tested with Halligan” to describe a specific test run
when:

- Halligan was actually used
- The Halligan version and test date are identified
- The probe suite or pack is identified by version, commit, checksum, or
  another unambiguous identifier where available
- The tested target and materially relevant configuration are identified
- A report or sufficient description of the methodology and scope is available
- The statement does not imply universal safety, regulatory compliance,
  certification, approval, or endorsement

Recommended form:

> Tested with Halligan [version] using [suite or pack identifier] on
> [YYYY-MM-DD]. Results apply only to the tested configuration.

“Tested with Halligan” is a factual self-attestation by the tester. It is not a
certification, warranty, safety rating, or endorsement by Foxxception LLC.

## Uses requiring permission

You must obtain written permission from Foxxception LLC before:

- Using Halligan or a confusingly similar term as the primary name of your
  company, product, hosted service, package, domain, event, or commercial
  offering
- Using the official Halligan logo as the primary branding for another product
  or service
- Claiming that a product, service, pack, implementation, or organization is
  “Official Halligan,” “Halligan Certified,” “Halligan Approved,” or endorsed
  by Foxxception LLC
- Selling merchandise featuring the official Halligan logo
- Registering a trademark, domain name, social-media account, or package name
  containing Halligan in a manner likely to imply official status

## Community use

Community members are encouraged to use the Halligan name when discussing,
teaching, contributing to, or supporting the project.

Community events, groups, and publications should identify themselves as
community-run when they are not operated by Foxxception LLC. The unmodified
Halligan logo may be used to refer to the official project, but it must not be
used in a way that suggests the surrounding project or event is official.

## Trademark attribution

When practical, use the following notice:

Do not use the registered trademark symbol, ®, unless Foxxception LLC has
obtained a trademark registration for the applicable mark.
